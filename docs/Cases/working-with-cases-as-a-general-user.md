# Working with Cases as a General User

## Before You Begin

Make sure the following are in place before submitting a support case as a General User:

* **CxCentral access.** You'll need a CxCentral account with credentials provided by your organization.
* **No access? Use email instead.** If you can't get into CxCentral, you can still submit a case using the support email address your organization provides. It enters the same support system.
* **Know your role.** As a General User, you can submit cases and view your own case history. Creating cases on behalf of others, editing across the organization, and managing contacts are admin functions—if you need those, contact your company admin.
* **Gather your details ahead of time.** Have your title, priority, case type, and a clear description ready, along with any files that help explain the issue, so you can complete the support form in one pass.
* **Check your attachments.** Confirm each file is within the size and format limits below before you upload.

***

## Limits and Constraints

Keep the following limits in mind when using Cases:

* **Attachment size.** Each file must be 10 MB or smaller.
* **Supported file types.** Only JPG, PNG, PDF, DOC, XLS, CSV, and ZIP files can be attached.
* **Preview limitations.** PDF, CSV, and DOC files can't be previewed in the attachment box—you'll need to download them to open them on your computer.
* **Recent cases on the home screen.** The CxCentral home page shows only your 7 most recent cases. To see your full history, click **View all Cases** to open the My Cases page.
* **Your own cases only.** You can view and track the cases tied to your email address. Visibility into all cases across the organization is limited to company admins.
* **Closed cases are locked.** A closed case can't be edited. Reopening it requires the appropriate role, so reach out to your company admin if a closed case needs changes.

***

## Step-by-Step Instructions <a href="#submit-a-case" id="submit-a-case"></a>

### Create a Case <a href="#submit-a-case" id="submit-a-case"></a>

1. Go to [https://portal.pronetx.com](https://portal.pronetx.com/).
2. Sign in with the credentials your organization provides.
3. Click **Support** in the top right corner of the screen.
4. Fill out the support request form and add attachments.
5. Click **Submit**.

A success message appears upon submission. You can navigate to the cases dashboard to view your case details.

!!! info ""
    If you can't access CxCentral, you can submit a support case using the support email address your organization provides.


***

### View Your Case <a href="#view-your-case" id="view-your-case"></a>

1. Log in to CxCentral.
2. The CxCentral home page displays the 7 most recent cases associated with your email address. To view all of your cases, click **View all Cases** or click **Cases** in the left navigation menu.
3. On the My Cases page, view your case Status, Reference Number, Title, Priority, Case Type, Creation Date, and Last Updated Date.
4. To view specific case details, click the case **Reference Number** or click the **ellipses** at the end of the row, and then **View**.

***

### View Case Details <a href="#view-case-details" id="view-case-details"></a>

The case details page shows a comprehensive overview and activity for your case. You can see the case status, date opened, organization, case number, priority, reporter, description, and timeline. Additionally, you can view all comments and attachments associated with your case.

***

###  Add Attachments <a href="#add-attachments" id="add-attachments"></a>

1. Click the **Reference Number** of a case to open the case details page.
2. Under the case details, upload images and files in the attachments box. You can drag and drop files or click the attachments box to select files from your computer.
3. Once attachments are added, you can preview the file in the attachment box. If a preview is not available, click the **Download button** to open the file on your computer.

!!! info ""
    **Note:** Some file types (PDF, CSV, and DOC) cannot be previewed in the attachment box but can be downloaded and opened on your computer. The maximum file size is 10 MB per file. Supported file types include JPG, PNG, PDF, DOC, XLS, CSV, and ZIP.


***

### Add Comments <a href="#add-comments" id="add-comments"></a>

1. Click the **Reference Number** of a case to open the case details page.
2. Under case detail, scroll down to the Comments box.
3. Enter your comment in the text box and click **Submit Comment**. The Comments section shows all conversation and notes for the case.

***

### Search for a Case <a href="#search-for-a-case" id="search-for-a-case"></a>

1. On the cases dashboard, enter the **Reference Number** into the Case Search box at the top of the page.
2. Use the **Status dropdown** at the top of the page to filter by case status or click the **calendar icon** to search using a date range.

***

### Bulk Edit Cases <a href="#bulk-edit-cases" id="bulk-edit-cases"></a>

1. On the cases dashboard, select the cases you want to edit.
2. Choose the status from the status dropdown for the selected cases. You can choose Open, Pending, Closed, In Progress, Waiting on Customer, or Waiting on Third Party.
3. A pop-up confirms the status change for the selected cases. Click **Confirm** to apply your changes.
