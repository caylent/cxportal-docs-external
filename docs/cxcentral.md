# CxCentral

### How CxCentral and CxPortal Fit Together

**CxCentral**          →            **CxPortal**

Your homepage              Where you work
CxCentral is your homepage. CxPortal is where you work.

Log in at portal.pronetx.com and you'll land on CxCentral; from there, navigate directly into CxPortal.

### What You Can Do in CxCentral
* Navigate to CxPortal and other Caylent tools via Quick Links or the left navigation menu
* Stay up to date with the latest product updates, new features, and improvements across your Caylent products
* Contact the Caylent team to report issues, request features, or send feedback — and track your open tickets

***

### Need Help?

!!! info ""
    Click **Request Support** in the upper right corner of the portal to open the support form. Once submitted you'll receive a confirmation email and your ticket will appear in the **Support Cases** widget on the homepage. For the full walkthrough, see [Submitting a Request](https://docs.caylent.com/cxportal/submitting-a-support-request/).
