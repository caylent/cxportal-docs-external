# Reviewing Change Requests

The **Change Requests** page lists submitted requests and their current status ("A list of change requests and their current status"). The heading shows the total count, e.g. **Change Requests (260)**. Urgent requests are listed before normal ones.

## The Change Requests Table

Columns: a selection checkbox, a **Bulk (n)** badge for bulk requests, **Change Type**, **Description**, **Requester**, **Criticality** (**Urgent** or **Normal**), **Date**, **Status**, and a row actions menu. Observed status values are **Pending**, **Approved**, and **Partially Rejected**. Use **Search by keyword...** to search and **Refresh data** to reload; click column headers to sort.

## Reviewing a Single Request

1. In the left sidebar, click **Change Management** > **Change Requests**.
1. Click the row menu (**Open menu**) on the request, then click **Review change**.
1. The **Change Request Details** dialog shows **Change Type**, **Requester**, **Requester ID**, **Creation Date**, **Description**, **Status**, **Criticality**, and the **Before State** / **After State** diff.
1. Click **Approve** to accept the change, **Reject** to decline it, or **Close** to leave it pending.

## Reviewing a Bulk Request

1. Click the row menu on a request with a **Bulk (n)** badge, then click **Review bulk change**.
1. The dialog shows the request metadata and a **Change Summary** (e.g. "4 records affected") with the values being applied.
1. Click **Approve Bulk** or **Reject Bulk** to act on the whole group, or click **View Individual Records** to step through each record before deciding.

## Approving or Rejecting Multiple Requests

1. Select the checkbox on each request you want to act on (or use the select-all checkbox in the header row).
1. Click **Approve Request** or **Reject Request** in the toolbar that appears.
1. Click **Clear** to deselect without acting.
