# Managing Scheduled Changes

The **Scheduled Changes** page lists approved changes that have not executed yet ("A list of scheduled changes and their execution dates").

## The Scheduled Changes Table

Columns: a **Bulk (n)** badge for bulk changes, **Change Type**, **Description**, **Requester**, **Approver**, and **Scheduled Date**, plus a row actions menu. Use **Search by keyword...** to search and **Refresh data** to reload; click column headers to sort.

## Reviewing or Cancelling a Scheduled Change

1. In the left sidebar, click **Change Management** > **Scheduled Changes**.
1. Click the row menu (**Open menu**) on the change, then click **Review change**.
1. The **Scheduled Change Details** dialog shows **Change Type**, **Requester**, **Requester ID**, **Creation Date**, **Scheduled Date**, **Description**, and the **Before State** / **After State** diff.
1. Click **Cancel** to cancel the scheduled change, or **Close** to leave it scheduled.

> **Warning:** Cancelling a scheduled change means it will not execute at its scheduled date.

# Updating a Record's Description

Records on all three pages have an editable description, shown in the **Description** column (records without one show **-**).
1. Click the row menu (**Open menu**) on the record.
1. Click **Update description**.
1. The **Update Description** dialog opens ("Add or edit the description for this entry.").
1. Enter or edit the **Description**.
1. Click **Save**, or **Cancel** to discard your edits.
