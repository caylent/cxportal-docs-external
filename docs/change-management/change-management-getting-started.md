# Getting Started with Change Management

## How to Access This Module

To access Change Management:
1. Log in to CxPortal.
1. Confirm the correct Amazon Connect instance is selected (shown in the header as **Instance:**).
1. In the left sidebar, click **Change Management**.
1. The module expands to show three sub-pages: **Audit Log**, **Change Requests**, and **Scheduled Changes**.

## Your First Task

Try it: review a change in the audit log (read-only — this is safe to do on any record).
1. In the left sidebar, click **Change Management** > **Audit Log**.
1. In the table, find a record you want to inspect.
1. At the right end of the row, click the row menu (**Open menu**).
1. Click **Review change**.
1. The **Audit Log Record Details** dialog opens, showing the record's metadata and a **Before State** / **After State** comparison.
1. Click **Close**.

## What to Do Next

- Filter and search the log → *Reviewing the Audit Log*
- Review pending requests → *Reviewing Change Requests*
- See upcoming changes → *Managing Scheduled Changes*
