# Getting Started with User Management

## Permissions

User Management is subject to role-based access. You must have a specific role designated by your organization to create roles and assign permissions per instance.

***

## How to Access This Module

To access User Management:

1. Go to CxCentral.
2. On the left side of the screen, click **Admin**.
3. In the left menu, click **Users**.

!!! info ""
    The Users page displays a list of users with the columns **Name** (email), **User ID**, **Login Methods** (Password/SSO with a status badge), and **Role**. The subtitle shows the user count. Above the list, a **Search Users** box and a **Filter by role** dropdown let you narrow the list.


***

### Your First Task

Try it: look up a user and view their details.

1. In the **Search Users** box, enter the user's email, or select a role in **Filter by role**.
2. Click the **View Details** (eye) icon on the user's row.
3. The user detail page opens, showing **User Details** (email and login methods) and **Role and Instance Access** (the user's role and the instances they can access).

***
