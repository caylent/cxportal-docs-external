# Getting Started with ACGR

## How to Access This Module

To access ACGR:

1. Go to **CxPortal**.
2. In the left navigation, select **ACGR**.
3. The module opens to **Dashboard**, **Playbooks**, and **Traffic Distributions**.

![](../../assets/images/Us0mArLoi1ihPowlDtze.png)

***

## Your First Task

The first task in ACGR is creating a **Traffic Distribution Group (TDG)**.

**Try it: Create your first Traffic Distribution Group**

1. In the left navigation, select **ACGR**, then click **Traffic Distributions**.
2. Click **+ Add** in the top-right corner.
3. Enter a **Name**.
4. (Optional) Add a **Description**.
5. Click **Create Traffic Distribution Group**.
6. The group appears in the list after creation. Updates are applied asynchronously, so it may take a few seconds before the group is visible.

!!! warning ""
    **Warning:** Name and description cannot be changed after creation. Verify all details are correct before clicking **Create Traffic Distribution Group**.


!!! info ""
    **Info:** After creating a Traffic Distribution Group, assign agents, traffic percentages, and phone numbers to the group. All phone numbers must belong to a TDG.


***

### What to Do Next

Now that you have created a Traffic Distribution Group, here is what to do next:

* Assign agents and phone numbers to your group → [**Managing Traffic Distribution Groups**](managing-traffic-distribution-groups-tdgs.md)
* Set how traffic is distributed between Regions → [**Managing Traffic Distribution Groups**](managing-traffic-distribution-groups-tdgs.md)
* Create a failover scenario → [**Managing Playbooks**](managing-playbooks.md)
* Trigger a failover when needed → [**Triggering a Failover**](triggering-a-failover.md)
